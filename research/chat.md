## 23 Nov 2026: Call with Carl, leather hat research findings reported

Deep research for Carl on getting very high-quality leather hats made outside large factories, without a 50-unit minimum. Full report: [Small-batch leather hat makers: research](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-d5c84a84-02f2-45ae-8126-d2c7dfd16d0f/a587ae14-5687-4f7e-bfe4-86956c8ec6eb).

### The three makers Carl found

- **Facopel** is real, but it's in Chiesina Uzzanese near **Pistoia**, about 50 km from Florence. It makes about **165,000 hats a year for more than 40 luxury brands**. The "50 units per colour, no one-offs" minimum came from Carl's own conversation with them and isn't published on their site, so it's worth getting in writing.
- **Ekzo (spoken as "Exo"), Armenia:** a small artisan making crocodile hats. It couldn't be verified anywhere online. Spelling correction from the call: E-K-Z-O. An Instagram handle would help verify it.
- **A B by Afruz, Iran:** a woman making very high-quality hats for herself. It couldn't be verified online, and an Instagram handle would help here too.

### Stronger new makers found

- **Nelson Were, Kent (UK):** handmakes leather caps and makes for other brands.
- **Owen Barry, Somerset (UK):** minimum of only **10 per style per colour**.
- **C & M Hatters and Albrizio, New York (USA):** **1 to 10 prototypes**, and production runs of **10 to 50**.
- **OJ Exclusive, Netherlands:** already makes one-off crocodile caps from **€795**.

### Business models, ranked

1. **Split the job:** a sample room perfects the pattern, Darian's Italian tanneries supply the leather, Darian's ratchets go in, a jeweller makes the magnetic peak piece, and a small workshop assembles.
2. **Small cap workshops** doing runs of 10 to 50.
3. **A bespoke atelier** for the top-end one-offs.

### One catch

- Crocodile and python need **CITES export papers for every border crossing**.
