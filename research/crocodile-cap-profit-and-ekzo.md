# Crocodile cap profit and Ekzo: follow-up findings

**Date:** 23 Nov 2026 · **Part of:** [Small-batch leather hat makers: research](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-d5c84a84-02f2-45ae-8126-d2c7dfd16d0f/a587ae14-5687-4f7e-bfe4-86956c8ec6eb) (full report: `small-batch-leather-hat-makers.md`)

Darian asked whether there is room for profit at OJ Exclusive's €795 crocodile cap price, and Carl asked for a re-check of the Armenian crocodile hat maker under the spelling "Ekzo".


> **Short answer:** There is profit in a crocodile cap, but not at $600. Making one or buying one costs about $550 to $1,000 (estimate). Comparable luxury crocodile caps sell for $2,950 to $6,750. So the crocodile cap should sit at $1,500 and up, with the magnetic jewellery carrying the $10,000+ tiers. Figures use the ECB rate of 20 Nov 2026, €1 = $1.099.

| Item | Sourced figure | Per cap (estimate) |

| Nile crocodile belly skin | $350 to $800 per skin wholesale, or $10 to $20 per cm of belly width (https://exoticleatherwholesale.com/exotic-leather-price/, June 2026); Italian crust from €12/cm (https://foxmycroco.shop/en/on_request/1/) | Croc brim and accents: about half to one skin, $175 to $400. Full croc cap: 1 to 2 skins, $350 to $1,600 |
| Leather needed | A cap takes about 3 to 4 sq ft (https://www.leather-patterns.com/post/the-complete-guide-to-leather-hat-making) | Used for the skin count above |
| Atelier labour | Small Tuscan leather contractor: €28.29/h full cost, €32.54/h quoted price (CNA Toscana study: https://toscana.cna.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/RICERCA-PROF.-AIELLO.pdf) | 8 to 12 hours for a one-off: €260 to €390 ($286 to $429) |
| CITES permits | Netherlands €60 per (re-)export permit (https://www.rvo.nl/onderwerpen/cites/document-aanvragen); UK £37 re-export, £67 import (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/cites-application-fees); Italy fee not found | About $66 to $150 per shipment |
| Ratchet, body leather, lining, shipping | Darian's own costs; shipping not sourced | Not included |
| Total before ratchet, body leather and shipping |  | Accent cap $527 to $895; full croc cap $702 to $2,095 |

All per-cap figures are **estimates** built from the sourced rates above. The labour hours are my assumption, and the one-time pattern and sampling cost is not included. Batches of 10 to 50 lower the skin waste and labour per cap.

Landed cost is €795 ($874) plus one €60 CITES permit ($66). Exchange rate: ECB reference rate of 20 Nov 2026, €1 = $1.099. Selling OJ's own design would not be an Ataei product. Their private-label route, with Darian's ratchet and peak jewellery added, is the version that could justify the higher prices.
